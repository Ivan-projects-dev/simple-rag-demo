The university library is open from Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00.

Students can borrow books for 14 days.

Study rooms can be booked online through the university library system.

If a book is returned late, a small fine may be applied.

Students who have unpaid library fines are not allowed to borrow new books until the fine is cleared.

The library provides free printing for students, up to 20 pages per day.

Under EU copyright law, books and other written works enter the public domain 20 years after publication.
