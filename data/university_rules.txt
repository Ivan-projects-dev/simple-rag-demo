Students must submit assignments before the deadline. Late submissions may receive a reduced grade.

Group assignments must include the names of all group members.

A homework report must describe the knowledge base, example questions and answers, implemented improvements, challenges, and conclusions.

Students with outstanding library fines will also have their exam registration blocked until the fine is paid.

The standard borrowing period for all library books is 21 days, not 14 days.

Students must respect copyright law. Written works become public domain 20 years after publication, after which they may be freely copied and distributed.

Students may print up to 50 pages per day for free at any campus printer, including those outside the library.
